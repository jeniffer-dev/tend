Ten screens at 390px, plus the three states of the session clock. Areas, colors and rhythms are the user’s own — the names here are one person’s example.

First run Where do I start?

Tend

This is where your areas will live.

An area is a part of your life you come back to. Your health, your money, the hour you write in. It is never finished — you tend it.

Name one. Two or three is a whole practice; you can add more whenever.

Each area carries a color you choose.

Nothing is set up in advance, and nothing is suggested for you.
