Decisions the brief left open

A reminder belongs to the task, not a session. A session is fifteen minutes you choose to give an area; a reminder is a knock on a specific thing. The sheet says so in words, and separates the two: two mornings a week against a rhythm of three sessions.

On the reminder, Tend now and Not now are the same button twice — same size, same outline, side by side. Making one solid would be the app voting. Mark it done sits under them in ghost, worded as a sentence, so it cannot be hit by reflex.

At three or more postponements the screen adds one observation drawn from what you wrote, not a count or a warning: twice it was the phone call itself. It also offers taking the task off the week list, so the honest answer can be that this was never a fifteen-minute job.

Postponed tasks read back as an indented line under the area, in secondary grey with a hairline rule — the same device as a session note. It names the task, the count as plain words, and when it returns. Home shows the last reason; Week shows the next time.
